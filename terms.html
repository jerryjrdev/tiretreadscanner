A guidance and record-keeping tool

Treadwise gives guidance and keeps records. It is not an inspection, it does not certify roadworthiness, tire condition or regulatory compliance, and it does not replace a calibrated tread depth gauge, a qualified tire or vehicle technician, or any inspection the law requires of you.

Decisions about whether a tire or vehicle is safe to use, repair or replace are yours, or those of the person responsible for the vehicle.

Readings are estimates

  • Depth scans and guided checks give estimates. Every reading shows how it was made (depth scan, guided check or gauge entry) and, for numbers, a plus-or-minus range.
  • Depth in 32nds appears only where the scan meets our accuracy bar on that kind of device. Otherwise the app shows a wear band (Good, Watch or Replace soon).
  • Depth scans are offered on supported iPhones. On iPad, the app offers the guided check and gauge entry.
  • Sunlight, dirt, water, snow or stones in the grooves, the angle and distance of the phone, and the part of the tread you scan all affect a reading. A scan measures only the patch of tread it sees.
  • Uneven-wear notes describe a possible cause. Have it checked.
  • Estimate. Verify with a gauge before you rely on any reading, especially near a guideline.

Guidelines and regulations

Guideline badges in the app name their source, for example "FMCSA 393.75 guideline" or "Common legal minimum 2/32". They are information, not legal advice. Rules differ between states and countries and change over time. You are responsible for knowing and following the rules that apply to you and your vehicles.

Inspection records

The daily inspection report (DVIR) is a record-keeping tool. Its checklist follows the items listed in 49 CFR 396.11, and the app keeps what you enter: answers, photos, signatures, repair sign-offs and next-driver reviews. Drivers and motor carriers remain responsible for their inspections, reports, repairs, signatures and record retention. Federal rules ask motor carriers to keep these reports for three months; keep your own copies (PDF or CSV) for as long as your obligations require.

A defect marked repaired in the app records what you or your mechanic entered. It is not a statement by us that the repair was made or that the vehicle may be driven.

Leak flags record that a leak was seen, where, how big, and with which photos. The app does not identify what is leaking.

Your records

Your records belong to you. They are stored on your device (see the privacy policy). This version does not sync them to iCloud or to any server, and we never hold a copy, so we cannot restore records that were deleted or lost, for example with Delete all data in Settings, by deleting the app or with a lost device. Export or share the reports you need to keep.
