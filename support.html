Readings and accuracy

How accurate is a tread scan?

Every reading is an estimate. We show depth in 32nds only on kinds of iPhone where our scans matched a calibrated digital gauge closely in testing: a typical error of 1/32 or less, and 9 in 10 readings within 2/32. The plus-or-minus next to each number is that measured range.

When a scan can't reach that bar, the app shows a wear band instead (Good, Watch or Replace soon) and offers a guided check. Near a guideline it asks you to confirm with a gauge. Estimate. Verify with a gauge.

Why do I see a wear band instead of a number?

Either your iPhone doesn't have a depth camera that meets the accuracy bar, or the scan conditions weren't good enough this time (bright sun, motion, dirt or water in the grooves, or the phone too close or too far). The app tells you which, and what to try: "Shade the tire with your body", "Clear the groove" or "Use the torch".

Does it work in bright sun or at night?

The front depth camera works well in shade and at night, and struggles in direct sun, which washes out its infrared light. Stand so your body shades the tread, or use a guided check or gauge entry. The torch helps photos at night.

How do I get the best scan on a steer tire or inner dual?

Scan two spots on steer tires (front and back of the visible tread); the guideline applies at the lowest point. Measure in a main groove, not on a wear bar or tie bar. If you can't reach an inner dual, scan at an angle or enter your gauge reading.

What do Good, Watch and Replace soon mean?

They compare the lowest groove with a guideline for that wheel position. For trucks and buses: FMCSA 393.75 guideline, 4/32 on steer tires and 2/32 on the rest, with an early warning at 6/32 and 4/32. For cars: the common legal minimum of 2/32, with an early warning at 4/32. Replace soon means at or below the guideline: verify with a gauge. See the tread depth chart for sources.

Devices

Which iPhones and iPads does it run on?

Any iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later. The tools you get depend on the hardware:

  • Depth scan with the front camera: iPhones with Face ID.
  • Depth scan with LiDAR: Pro iPhones with a LiDAR scanner.
  • Guided check (penny, quarter or wear bar) and gauge entry: every iPhone and iPad, including iPhone SE.

The first time you open the app it shows what your device supports. No permission is needed for that check.

Can I scan with an iPad?

Not yet. Depth scans are tuned for iPhone, and we haven't measured iPad cameras against a gauge, so on iPad the app offers the guided check and gauge entry. Everything else (tire maps, history, inspections, reports and settings) works on iPad.

Do I need to look at the screen while scanning?

No. With the front camera the screen faces the tire, so the app coaches you with spoken cues and vibration ("closer", "hold still") and plays a chime when it's done.

Trial, billing and restore

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Both plans, $13.99 per week or $129.99 per year, start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. The trial can be used once across both plans. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the plan you chose starts and renews automatically. The app shows whether a trial is available to you before you subscribe. If you already allow notifications for the app, it reminds you two days before the trial ends.

How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Treadwise Pro and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also do this from your account in the App Store app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the end of the period.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app and tap Restore Purchases. It's on the subscription screen and in Settings. Your records stay on the device where you made them.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I run more than 10 units. Can I use it?

A subscription covers up to 10 units. Tap Settings › Need more than 10 units? in the app, or write to qidientlabs@gmail.com with your fleet size, and we'll tell you what's coming for larger fleets.

Your data

Where are my records stored?

On your device only. This version has no iCloud sync and no account with us, and we can't see your records. Settings shows them as saved on this device. Scans, records and reports work fully offline. To check your subscription, the app talks to RevenueCat, our subscription service provider, which receives your purchase history under a random ID but never your records. See the privacy policy.

Do my records sync to my other devices?

No. Records stay on the device where you made them. Your subscription works on every device signed in to the same Apple Account: open the app there and tap Restore Purchases. Share a PDF report or a CSV export from the first device if you need the record somewhere else.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings › Delete all data in the app and type "delete" to confirm. It removes every unit, reading, inspection record, photo and signature from the device, and your subscription stays active. Deleting the app removes its data too. Neither can be undone, so share any reports you need to keep first. A device backup can still hold a copy until that backup is deleted.

Can I export my records?

Yes. The Reports tab keeps your DVIR and tire report PDFs, ready to share, and can export your readings and inspections as a CSV file for a spreadsheet. Exports go only where you send them.

Inspections (DVIR)

What does the inspection report include?

The checklist follows the items in 49 CFR 396.11 (brakes, steering, lights and reflectors, tires, horn, wipers, mirrors, coupling devices, wheels and rims, emergency equipment), with a tire step, a leak step, a photo on every defect, the driver's signature, an optional repair sign-off and next-driver review, and a PDF you can share. A car checklist is included too. To change the items, copy a checklist in Settings and edit the copy; past reports keep the items they were signed with.

Does a report from the app meet my obligations?

The app is a record-keeping tool: it keeps the record, and you stay responsible for what the rules require of your operation, including how long you keep reports. 49 CFR 396.11 asks carriers to keep reports, repair sign-offs and driver reviews for three months, and it allows electronic records. It does not require a report when no defect is found, and it doesn't apply to carriers running a single vehicle, but many owner-operators keep a daily record anyway. Read the current rule text or our printable checklist.
