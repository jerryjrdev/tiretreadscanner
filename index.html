Worn tread is easy to miss.

It wears a little at a time, so most people find out from a tire shop, an inspector or a wet road. If you have been meaning to check, you are in good company.

A depth scan, step by step

  1. Hold the phone 6 to 12 inches from the treadThat is 15 to 30 cm. Aim at the part of the tread you can see best.
  2. Hold still until the chimeThe app gathers about 2 to 3 seconds of depth data.
  3. Read each grooveOuter, center and inner depth, the lowest point, a wear band and the guideline for that wheel position.
  4. Confirm near the lineWhen a reading sits close to a guideline, the app asks you to check it with a gauge and keeps your number.

What your iPhone can do

Phone depth sensors are small, so we hold them to a bar. Depth in 32nds appears only where our scan matches a calibrated gauge closely enough on that kind of iPhone. Everywhere else you get a wear band and a guided check.
