Privacy policy

The short version: no account, no ads, no tracking, and nothing sold. What you record stays on your device, and you can delete it from Settings at any time. The app sends out only your subscription details and a few technical details, and RevenueCat keeps the subscription record for us under a random ID.

Effective November 25, 2026

Who we are

Treadwise is published by QIDIENT GLOBAL LTD ("we"), 12 Jabi Road, Jabi, Abuja, Nigeria. We are responsible for (the "controller" of) the personal data we handle as described in this policy; Apple and GitHub are responsible for the data they handle themselves. This policy covers the Treadwise app for iPhone and iPad and this website. Questions go to qidientlabs@gmail.com.

No account

You don't create an account to use Treadwise, and we don't ask for your name, email address or phone number. Names you type in the app (for example the default inspector, a mechanic or the next driver on an inspection record) stay with your records on your device.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored in the app's storage on your device:

  • your vehicles and units, their tire maps and the details you enter (unit number, plate, VIN, make and model, odometer or hours);
  • tire readings: the depth per groove or wear band, the method used, the confidence, notes and a photo of the tread;
  • daily inspection records (DVIRs), with checklist answers, photos and the signatures you draw or type;
  • leak flags, defects and repair notes, with their photos and the mechanic's name and signature;
  • your settings, including a company name and logo for PDF reports;
  • the PDF reports and CSV files you create.

Depth scans are processed on your device. Depth data from the front (TrueDepth) or rear (LiDAR) camera is used only to measure tread depth. The app keeps the result of a scan, not the raw depth data. The app never sends depth data or photos to us or to any third party (RevenueCat included). Photos leave your device only in reports, exports or backups you make yourself, and depth data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining.

iCloud sync is not active yet

This version of the app does not sync. There is no iCloud copy and no server copy: your records stay on the device where you made them, and Settings shows them as saved on this device. Your subscription still works on every device signed in to the same Apple Account.

If a later version turns on iCloud sync, it will use your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit. We cannot read that database. Sync would run only while iCloud is on for the app in your device settings, its storage would count against your iCloud plan, and Delete all data would then remove the iCloud copy too. We will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.

What we receive

The app sends no usage analytics (what you do in the app) anywhere and makes no network requests to servers we run. Apart from Apple's own services, such as the App Store and its purchase sheet, and the web pages you choose to open from it, the one service it talks to is RevenueCat, to check your subscription (see "Subscriptions and RevenueCat" below). Through RevenueCat we can see subscription records under a random ID, with some of the technical details listed below (never the device identifier or your IP address), and nothing else. Because the app and this website don't track you, they don't respond differently to browser Do Not Track signals.

To help with troubleshooting, the app writes short app events (for example "scan completed" or "trial started") to your device's own system log. They carry only event names, choices and counts, stay on your device and are not sent to us. They never contain photos, names, notes or anything else you typed.

If we add usage statistics in a later version, they will be anonymous counts that help us improve scanning (for example "scan completed" or "scan needed a retake"). They will not include your photos, readings, reports, names or location, will not be linked to you or used for advertising, and this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics in a form that does not identify you. You can turn this off in your device's privacy settings.

No tracking, no ads, nothing sold

The app has no advertising, no usage-analytics or tracking SDKs and no ad attribution, never asks to track you and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The only third-party code that sends data is RevenueCat's, for your subscription and the subscription counts described below. We do not sell personal data, and we share it only as this policy describes, never for advertising.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Apple handles every payment, under Apple's own privacy policy. We never see your card or other payment details.

We use RevenueCat, Inc., a subscription service provider, to check and restore your subscription and to manage our plans and prices. RevenueCat handles this data on our behalf, under its data processing terms with us. Under those terms RevenueCat may process the data only on our instructions, to provide its service to us, and must protect it with appropriate technical and organizational security measures.

What RevenueCat receives, from the app and, for your purchase history, also directly from Apple, which tells RevenueCat about renewals, cancellations, billing problems and refunds:

  • a random ID the app creates on your device. It doesn't contain your name, email address or Apple Account details, and we don't ask for any of those. Your subscription record is still personal data, and the rights below apply to it;
  • your App Store purchase history for Treadwise: the plan, price and currency, and the dates and status of purchases, trials, renewals, cancellations, billing problems and refunds, with the transaction records Apple signs;
  • technical details sent with each request: device model, iOS version, app version, language and region settings, App Store country and an identifier that iOS gives to apps from the same developer on your device (not the advertising identifier). RevenueCat has told us that it uses that identifier only to handle the request and doesn't store it. The app also sends whether you allow apps to ask to track you (the app never asks), and RevenueCat keeps that with your record.

Your IP address reaches RevenueCat with each request, as with any internet connection. RevenueCat uses it only to estimate your country when the App Store doesn't provide one, and says it doesn't keep it.

RevenueCat never receives your readings, photos, vehicles, inspection records, signatures, the names or notes you type, your onboarding answers or your location (beyond the country estimate above).

Why: to confirm that your trial or subscription is active and unlock the app, to restore it on a new device, to apply offer codes, to show the plans and prices on the subscription screen (which lets us change or compare plans and prices without an app update; the App Store always shows the price you will pay before you confirm), and to count trials, renewals, cancellations and refunds so we understand how the app is doing.

How long: RevenueCat keeps these records while we use it for the app, so your subscription keeps working and our accounts stay accurate, unless you ask us to delete them. If we stop using RevenueCat, we ask it to delete them.

Deleting them: write to qidientlabs@gmail.com with the Order ID from Apple's receipt email (you can also find it at reportaproblem.apple.com), so we can find your record without asking for your name or Apple Account. We delete it from RevenueCat and confirm within 30 days. Deleting the record doesn't cancel your subscription: cancel it in your App Store account settings first. While the app stays installed it keeps the same random ID, and RevenueCat creates a new record under that ID the next time the app checks your subscription, whether or not you subscribe. To stop that, delete the app before you write to us. If you install it again later, it starts with a new random ID.

RevenueCat's own privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Sharing reports and exports

When you share a PDF report, a CSV export or a photo, it goes only where you send it, using your device's share sheet (for example Mail, Messages, AirDrop or Files). A PDF contains what you put in the record: names, signatures, photos, readings and your company name and logo. By default it also carries a small "Made with Treadwise" footer with a QR code, which you can turn off in Settings.

Contacting support from the app

Settings › Contact support opens a draft email in your Mail app with the app version, your device model and iOS version filled in. You see the whole draft before you send it, and the app attaches nothing else.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete all data removes every unit, reading, inspection record, photo and signature from the device. You type "delete" to confirm, and it can't be undone, so share any PDF reports you need to keep first. Your subscription stays active.

Delete all data doesn't touch your subscription record at RevenueCat; to have that deleted, see "Subscriptions and RevenueCat" above.

Deleting the app also removes its data. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, that backup can hold a copy of the app's data until the backup itself is deleted.

Inspection records are yours to keep. If the rules for your operation require you to keep DVIRs (49 CFR 396.11 asks motor carriers to keep them for three months), keep copies before you delete anything.

Emailing us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed or when you ask.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. in the United States. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, under its own privacy statement; we don't receive those logs.

Children

Your rights

Because your records stay on your device, you can view, export and delete them yourself at any time. For anything we hold (such as an email you sent us, or your subscription record at RevenueCat), you can ask us for a copy, to correct it, to delete it, to limit how we use it, or to send you your subscription record in a common machine-readable format. For your subscription record, include the Order ID from Apple's receipt email so we can find it. Write to qidientlabs@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Your right to object: where we rely on our legitimate interests (counting trials, renewals, cancellations and refunds, comparing plans and prices, and answering emails), you can object at any time by writing to the same address. We will then stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests. RevenueCat counts every subscription record it holds, so for subscription records we stop by deleting your record as described in "Deleting them" above.

Legal bases and transfers

This section applies where data protection law asks for it, for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom and Nigeria.

  • Subscription records, and the technical details sent with them: we use them to provide the subscription you ask for, which means confirming, unlocking and restoring it and applying offer codes (performance of a contract). We also use them to count trials, renewals, cancellations and refunds and to compare plans and prices, because we have a legitimate interest in understanding how the app is doing and in pricing it well.
  • Emails you send us: we use them to answer you, because we have a legitimate interest in answering questions about the app. When you write about your subscription, or about subscribing, we use them to perform or prepare that contract.
  • Whether you have to provide data: no law requires you to. Checking your subscription with RevenueCat is part of how the app works, and without it the app can't confirm, unlock or restore your subscription. Writing to us is up to you.
  • No automated decisions: we don't make decisions about you based only on automated processing, and we don't profile you. If we compare plans and prices, which plans a new customer sees is chosen at random, and the App Store always shows the price before you confirm.
  • Where your data is handled: RevenueCat and the service providers it uses handle subscription records in the United States. Email to our Gmail address is handled by Google, which may store it in the United States and other countries. We are based in Nigeria and read subscription records and emails from there. For subscription records from the EU, the UK and Switzerland, RevenueCat's data processing terms with us include the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, with the UK addendum. They are published at revenuecat.com/dpa, and you can ask us for a copy at qidientlabs@gmail.com.

If you think we have not handled your data properly, please tell us first. You can also complain to a data protection authority: the one where you live (in the EU, your national supervisory authority; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office) or, because we are based in Nigeria, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
